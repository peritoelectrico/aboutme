Hello! My professional journey began in the Cuban Army, where I served as a Radio Station Chief. After completing my military service, I pursued a degree in Telecommunications and Electronics Engineering at the University City José Antonio Echeverría (CUJAE) while working as a Radio Technician.

In 1998, I moved to the United States and started my career as an independent contractor, repairing commercial and industrial electrical equipment for hotels throughout Las Vegas, Nevada. My passion for automation led me to Paragon Technologies, where I led teams in the design, development, implementation, and troubleshooting of advanced industrial control systems and electronic solutions.

I later joined Elite Line Services (Daifuku) as a Controls System Lead, where I managed controls teams and played a key role in the successful startup of Amazon's LAS7 fulfillment center—one of the company's highly automated robotic sorting facilities.

Today, I serve as a Senior Process Control Engineer with Georgia-Pacific at the Sweetwater Gypsum Complex in Texas. In this role, I lead automation, controls, electrical, and multidisciplinary engineering initiatives, supporting the development, startup, and optimization of one of the most technologically advanced gypsum manufacturing facilities in North America. My focus is on integrating advanced automation systems, improving operational efficiency, enhancing product quality, and driving innovation across manufacturing operations.

Throughout my career, I have been passionate about building high-performing teams, solving complex technical challenges, and bringing world-class industrial automation projects from concept to successful operation.

Throughout my career, I have embraced a philosophy of continuous improvement, knowledge sharing, and value creation. I believe that engineering excellence is achieved by combining technical expertise with principled leadership, empowering teams to innovate, solve complex problems, and deliver results that create lasting value for customers, employees, and the business.
