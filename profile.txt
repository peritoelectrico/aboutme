Hello! My professional journey began in the Cuban Army, where I served as a Radio Station Chief. Following my military service, I earned a degree in Telecommunications and Electronics Engineering from the University City José Antonio Echeverría (CUJAE) while working as a Radio Technician, building a strong foundation in communications, electronics, and problem-solving.
In 1998, I relocated to the United States and began my career as an independent contractor, specializing in the repair and maintenance of commercial and industrial electrical systems for major hotel properties throughout Las Vegas, Nevada. Driven by a passion for automation and technology, I later joined Paragon Technologies, where I led teams responsible for the design, development, implementation, and commissioning of advanced industrial control systems and automation solutions.
I subsequently joined Amazon.com-Inc (ELS-Daifuku Webb) as a Controls System Lead, managing controls teams and contributing to the successful startup of Amazon's LAS7 Fulfillment Center, one of the company's highly automated robotic sorting facilities. This experience further strengthened my expertise in large-scale automation, systems integration, and operational excellence.
Today, I serve as a Senior Process Control Engineer with Georgia-Pacific's Sweetwater Gypsum Complex in Texas. In this role, I lead automation, controls, electrical, and multidisciplinary engineering initiatives that support the operation, optimization, and continuous improvement of one of the most technologically advanced gypsum manufacturing facilities in North America. My responsibilities include driving automation strategies, implementing innovative technologies, improving reliability and efficiency, enhancing product quality, and supporting capital projects from concept through commissioning.
Throughout my career, I have been passionate about building high-performing teams, solving complex technical challenges, and delivering world-class industrial automation projects. I believe engineering excellence is achieved through a combination of technical expertise, principled leadership, and a commitment to continuous improvement. By fostering collaboration, sharing knowledge, and empowering others to innovate, I strive to create lasting value for employees, customers, and the organizations I serve.
