ℹ️ Note: These calculations are intended for linear-motion systems driven by a rotary motor + gearbox — e.g. rack-and-pinion drives, ball/lead screws, belt & pulley conveyors, winches, or any application where a rotary output is converted into linear travel. The Rack Scale field represents the linear distance traveled per 1 revolution of the gearbox output shaft (pinion circumference, screw lead, pulley circumference, etc.). Linear speed and thrust force are derived from this value.
🔧 PLC Tip (Studio 5000): This value corresponds to the Conversion Constant in the Axis Properties → Scaling tab of a Motion Group axis. It defines the Position Units per Revolution of the gearbox output shaft (e.g. cm/Rev or in/Rev). Keep your selected unit here consistent with the axis Position Units configured in the controller.
