Transformers, Motors & Mechanical Load Sizing

📘 Student Guide & Formulas

  • kW = kVA × PF × (Efficiency/100)
  • kVAR = √(kVA² - (kVA × PF)²)
  • Phase Selection: Documents your system type. The kVA→kW conversion math remains identical for 1φ and 3φ.
  • Efficiency: Accounts for core/copper losses in transformers or mechanical losses in motors.
