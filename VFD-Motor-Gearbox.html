Student Reference: Core Formulas & Live Calculation

All values below update automatically as you change input parameters. Formulas are standard for 3-phase induction motor analysis.

1. Synchronous Speed (RPM)

Synchronous speed is the theoretical speed of the motor's magnetic field
\( n_s = \frac{120 \times f_{base}}{P} \)
Where \( f_{base} = 60Hz \) (base supply frequency), \( P = \) pole count
Live Calculation: \( \frac{120 \times 60}{4} = 1800.0 \, \text{RPM} \)

2. Slip (RPM & Percentage)

Slip is the difference between synchronous speed and actual rated speed, caused by load
\( s_{RPM} = n_s - n_{rated} \)
\( s_{\%} = \left( \frac{s_{RPM}}{n_s} \right) \times 100 \)
Live Calculation: \( 1800.0 - 1765 = 35.0 \, \text{RPM} \, (1.94\%) \)

3. Input Power (kVA)

Total apparent power input to the motor (3-phase)
\( kVA = \frac{\sqrt{3} \times V \times I_{FLA}}{1000} \)
Where \( \sqrt{3} \approx 1.732 \), \( V = \) input voltage, \( I_{FLA} = \) full load amps
Live Calculation: \( \frac{1.732 \times 480 \times 18.7}{1000} = 15.55 \, \text{kVA} \)

4. Output Power (HP & kW)

Actual mechanical power output of the motor, accounting for losses
\( HP = kVA \times PF \times Efficiency \times 1.341 \)
(1 kW = 1.341 HP)
Live Calculation: \( 15.55 \times 0.88 \times 0.915 \times 1.341 = 17.7 \, \text{HP} \)

5. Rated Torque (lb-ft)

Rotational force produced at rated speed
\( T = \frac{HP \times 5252}{n_{rated}} \)
Live Calculation: \( \frac{17.7 \times 5252}{1765} = 52.7 \, \text{lb-ft} \)

6. V/Hz Ratio

Volts per Hertz ratio required to maintain constant magnetic flux in the motor
Standard Mode: \( \frac{V_{input}}{60Hz} \)
Split Mode: \( \frac{V_{input}/2}{60Hz} \) (motor rewired to see half input voltage)
Live Calculation (Standard): \( \frac{480}{60} = 8.0 \, \text{V/Hz} \)
Live Calculation (Split): \( \frac{240}{60} = 4.0 \, \text{V/Hz} \)

7. Maximum Speed at 120Hz

Maximum achievable speed at full 120Hz operation, accounting for constant slip
\( n_{max} = \frac{120 \times 120}{P} - s_{RPM} \)
Live Calculation: \( \frac{14400}{4} - 35.0 = 3565 \, \text{RPM} \)

8. Torque Above Base Speed (Field Weakening)

Above base speed (60Hz standard / ~118Hz split), voltage is capped, so torque drops to maintain constant HP
\( T = \frac{HP \times 5252}{n_{actual}} \)
Live Calculation at 75Hz: \( \frac{17.7 \times 5252}{2214} = 42.0 \, \text{lb-ft} \)
