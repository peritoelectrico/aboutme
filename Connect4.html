How to Play

  • Drag with Right-click (or Left-click) to rotate the 3D board view.
  • Hover your mouse over columns to see a preview chip.
  • Left-click on a column to drop your piece.
  • Align 4 chips horizontally, vertically, or diagonally to win.
  • Starter rule: The starting player alternates each match.
